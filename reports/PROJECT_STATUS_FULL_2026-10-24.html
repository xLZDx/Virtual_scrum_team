2. How the project is laid out and where we are on the map

The project is a unified virtual engineering organization: Virtual Scrum Team as the single SDLC authority (ten phases), isolated workers that never approve their own output, independent reviewers, a gate with signed receipts and a state machine. The blueprint roadmap runs S0-S9. Design baseline 1.0 (2026-10-24) replaces S0-S9 for near-term planning only; the rest follows the blueprint.

Indicative scale: 10 sprints on the map; S0 partial, S1 code finished, S2-S9 not started. It counts stages, not effort.
