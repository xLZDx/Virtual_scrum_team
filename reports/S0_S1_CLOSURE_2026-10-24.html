Defects fixed after the independent review

  • The retry bound counted PASS receipts. Re-attesting at a new head could be refused and the story stuck. The bound now counts only non-PASS receipts of the current epoch.
  • Spending exactly the budget cap. Any later lease call committed a terminal marker and cancelled an already complete APPROVE. The budget now blocks only on overspend.
  • Pre-claiming supervisor receipt ids. A worker could claim a supervisor marker id and break the supervisor. Worker receipt ids with the prefix sup- are rejected.
